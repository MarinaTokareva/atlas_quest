Support

Answers to common questions, and how to get in touch.

Contact us

To ask a question, report a problem or point out a wrong fact, open a request on our public support board. You'll need a free GitHub account.

Open a support request
Support requests are public. Please don't include your name, email address, Apple Account details or purchase receipts.

Subscriptions

What do I get with Premium?

Unlimited quizzes, all 7 quiz modes, practice for the countries you got wrong ("My Mistakes"), and quizzes of up to 40 questions. The free version includes 5 quizzes a day in the Flags and Capitals modes, plus the whole Learn and Progress sections.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Atlas Quest, tap the gear icon on the Play tab, then tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel?

In the app, tap the gear icon on the Play tab, then Manage Subscription. You can also cancel in the iPhone Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using the app

When do my free quizzes reset?

At midnight, local time on your device.

What does "mastered" mean?

A country is mastered after you answer 3 questions about it correctly in a row. A wrong answer resets that count.

Why is my progress missing on my other device?

Your progress is stored only on the device where you played, so it doesn't sync between devices. It's included in your device backups, and restoring a backup restores it.

How do I start over?

Tap the gear icon on the Play tab, then Reset Progress. This deletes your quiz history and mastery and can't be undone.
