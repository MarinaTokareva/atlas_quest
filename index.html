Learn the world, one flag at a time.

Atlas Quest is a geography quiz app for iPhone and iPad. Learn the flags, capitals, currencies and continents of 194 countries, and see your progress as you go.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Map quizzes

Find countries on the world map, or name the one that's highlighted. Plus US states, Canadian provinces and Ukrainian oblasts.

12 quiz types

Flags, capitals, currencies, continents, neighbours, languages, driving side, dialling codes and more.

Daily 10 and challenges

A daily quiz built from your weak spots, plus Survival and Against the Clock with Game Center leaderboards.

Learn as you play

Every answer comes with the country's capital, currency and continent, plus notes on tricky facts.

Study mode

Browse and search every country, or flip through flashcards one continent at a time.

Track your progress

Day streaks, accuracy, mastery for each continent, and a list of the countries you need to practise.
